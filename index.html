Family Setup

For the family — fill this in once. Everything saves on this device only and makes the big buttons work. Leave a box empty if it is not needed yet.

Family Helpers — who he can call and ask for help

The FIRST person in this list is the main helper: the "Ask for Help" and quick-message buttons go to them. The "I AM STUCK" message buttons go to EVERYONE in this list — one button for each person, so he can send the same stuck message to all of them, one by one. Add a sister or anyone else here and they get their own big Call button on the home screen, and appear everywhere a helper can be chosen. Take someone off with the button under their name.

His Doctors — everyone he sees

Each doctor gets a big CALL button and a DIRECTIONS button on the Health & Hospital screen, and a RIDE button if the address is filled in. Add as many as he has — heart doctor, family doctor, eye doctor, anyone.

His Medicines

The medicine name and when he takes it, in plain words — "Morning", "Night", "With food". The list is only for remembering; the doctor's words always come first.

His Appointments

One line each, in plain words — for example "Tuesday 10 am — Dr. Khan, heart check". The app shows them on the Health & Hospital screen and can read them aloud, but it cannot ring or remind by itself.

My Places — his regular websites

Each one becomes a big button on the home screen. Examples: his email inbox page, a news site, YouTube, his bank.

Remote help: the SHOW MY SCREEN screen now has two routes. Route 1 is built into this app (computer only): he taps START, chooses Entire screen, and family watches live in this same app from the link he sends — view only, nobody can click for him. This app does not record or save the screen video. A connection service is used to connect the two browsers; the live screen is sent using WebRTC. Route 2 is AnyDesk: install AnyDesk (free) once on his phone and computer, turn on unattended access, and keep the password with family only — never write it in this app. After that one-time setup, family can connect and help from anywhere, with control; on some phones he may be asked to tap Allow. This app never sees or stores that password.

His documents: Copies on this shelf live only on this device — they are not a backup. For the real collection, put his documents in Google Drive or OneDrive once (they then appear on his new computer and phone by themselves), and use AnyDesk file transfer to pull anything else from his old computer. This app cannot collect files from his computer by itself — no web page can.

Where is he — family location: This web app cannot watch his location in the background or send automatic leaving-home alerts — no web page can. The automatic leaving-home and coming-home alerts will come with the Android version of this same app (the family's own app — no third-party service). Until then, his in-app way is the SEND MY LOCATION button (one tap prepares a WhatsApp message with his position to the helper he picks, while the app is open) and the I AM GOING OUT / I AM HOME NOW buttons.
