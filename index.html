Family Setup

For Zee and family — fill this in once. Everything saves on this device only and makes the big buttons work. Leave a box empty if it is not needed yet.

Family Helpers — who he can call and ask for help

The FIRST person in this list is the main helper: the "Ask for Help", "I AM STUCK" and quick-message buttons go to them. Add a sister or anyone else here and they get their own big Call button on the home screen, and appear everywhere a helper can be chosen. Take someone off with the button under their name.

My Places — his regular websites

Each one becomes a big button on the home screen. Examples: his email inbox page, a news site, YouTube, his bank.

Remote help: install AnyDesk (free) once on his phone and computer, turn on unattended access, and keep the password with family only — never write it in this app. After that one-time setup, family can connect and help from anywhere; on some phones he may be asked to tap Allow. The SHOW MY SCREEN button on the home screen only opens AnyDesk; this app never sees or stores that password.

His documents: Copies on this shelf live only on this device — they are not a backup. For the real collection, put his documents in Google Drive or OneDrive once (they then appear on his new computer and phone by themselves), and use AnyDesk file transfer to pull anything else from his old computer. This app cannot collect files from his computer by itself — no web page can.

Where is he — family location: This web app cannot watch his location in the background or send automatic leaving-home alerts — no web page can. The automatic leaving-home and coming-home alerts will come with the Android version of this same app (the family's own app — no third-party service). Until then, his in-app way is the SEND MY LOCATION button (one tap prepares a WhatsApp message with his position to the helper he picks, while the app is open) and the I AM GOING OUT / I AM HOME NOW buttons.
